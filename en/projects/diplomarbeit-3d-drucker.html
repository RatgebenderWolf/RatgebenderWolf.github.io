Diploma thesis — HTBLuVA St. Pölten

Infinity 3D Printer — development of a 3D printer

Completed 06.2020 — 05.2021

About

In this project a belt 3D printer was designed and manufactured. The printer can print infinitely far in one direction and start a new print immediately after finishing one. It also has four different extruders that are swapped fully automatically. This makes it possible to produce very long prototypes, such as the front bumper of a truck. In addition, several materials can be processed within a single print, allowing parts that are soft at the core and hard on the outside.

Results

  • Design of the 3D printer
  • 3D CAD design
  • Manufacturing documentation
  • Product structure and routing plan
  • Cost-effectiveness analysis
  • Visualisation
  • Prototype construction started
