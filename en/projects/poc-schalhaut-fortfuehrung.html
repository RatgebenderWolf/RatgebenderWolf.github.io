Bachelor study project 4 — continuation of project 2

Proof of concept: quality detection of used plywood

Completed 03.2025 — 07.2025

About

In the second dual study project of the Smart Engineering programme, a concept was developed for collecting data to train an AI. The goal is to check the quality of used plywood facing automatically. The project serves as a proof of concept and is intended to show what is possible with image data.

Project objectives

  • Applying project management methods
  • Creating a concept for data processing and AI training
  • Preparing data and starting to label it

Research questions

  • How is the data labelled?
  • Which neural network architectures are used?
