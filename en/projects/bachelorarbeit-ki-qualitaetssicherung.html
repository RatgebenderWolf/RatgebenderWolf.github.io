Bachelor's thesis — Smart Engineering programme

Automating quality assurance for rental formwork using artificial intelligence

Development and evaluation of AI models for detecting defects on formwork facing surfaces

Completed Submitted 09.2025

About

This thesis deals with data collection, preparation and annotation as well as the training and evaluation of AI models. It is based on image data of Doka Xlife formwork panels that were returned from construction sites and subsequently cleaned with high-pressure equipment.

The aim of the thesis is to investigate the use of artificial intelligence for automated surface inspection of formwork elements. The focus lies on capturing and annotating image data of damaged formwork facing. Building on this dataset, AI models are trained and evaluated that are intended to locate and classify defects automatically. The work is thus intended to lay the groundwork for future developments in automated quality control and automated reconditioning of formwork elements.

Publication planned for 2031.
