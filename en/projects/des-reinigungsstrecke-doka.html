Bachelor study project 1

Discrete event simulation of a formwork element processing line

Completed 08.2023 — 02.2024

About

The project focuses on a thorough analysis of a newly installed cleaning line. It follows a comprehensive approach comprising the recording of process data and the creation of a simulation. This simulation is evaluated in order to identify potential improvements. A central aspect is optimising the line's performance so that it can be assessed effectively against other comparable lines and so that capacity can be controlled more efficiently.

The main goals include capturing current data such as throughput time and setup times of the line. This data forms the basis for an in-depth analysis of the processes, specifically looking for opportunities to optimise them. A discrete event simulation (DES) of the line is built in order to model the dynamics and interactions of the process steps. The results of this simulation are evaluated to gain insights that form the basis for concrete improvement measures. Defining five specific process steps ensures that the simulation gives a realistic representation of how the line functions.

Results

The project concludes with a comprehensive simulation of the line, concrete proposals for optimising the processes, and a body of collected data that provides insight into the line's performance and efficiency. These results form the basis for future decisions on developing the cleaning line further and increasing its productivity.
