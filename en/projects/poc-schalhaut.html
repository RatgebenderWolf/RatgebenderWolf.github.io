Bachelor study project 2

Proof of concept: quality detection of used plywood

Completed 02.2024 — 07.2024

About

The project aims to improve the quality of used plywood facing through the use of artificial intelligence and image processing. The idea is to develop automated systems that can detect and classify surface defects precisely. The long-term goal is to increase the efficiency of defect detection and reduce manual rework.

Goals

  • Defining levels of automation for the reconditioning of formwork elements
  • Finding technologies for quality detection and identifying promising candidates
  • Building prototypes for data collection and collecting data

Results

  • A set of defined levels of automation
  • Quality detection: evaluation of various detection methods and selection of one method
  • Budget for prototypes was approved; the specific material has yet to be decided
